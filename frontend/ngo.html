An administrator checks every NGO before it can post needs or claim donations.

A claim moves through five steps. You confirm the pickup plan within 48 hours, the donor records the pickup, then you mark it delivered and confirm receipt.

To claim items, press Find donations next to a need. You'll see donations ranked for it and choose how many to take.
