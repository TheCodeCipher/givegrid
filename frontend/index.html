How it works

  1. List what you are giving

    Say what it is, how many, its condition and which city it is in.

  2. NGOs see ranked matches

    Same category first, then donations that cover the whole need, then the nearest ones.

  3. Claim and confirm within 48 hours

    A claim holds the items for two days. If not enough is left, GiveGrid offers what is.

  4. Hand over and confirm receipt

    Pickup, delivery and receipt are recorded in a history that cannot be edited.

Built so nothing goes missing

Nothing promised twice

When two NGOs claim the last items at the same moment, only one gets them. The other is offered what is left.

Holds that expire

Claims not confirmed within 48 hours are released automatically, so items never sit reserved for nobody.

A history no one can rewrite

Every step of every donation is recorded permanently. The database itself refuses changes to it.
